Demon Slayer : Kimetsu no Yaiba

Demon Slayer is a popular Japanese anime that follows Tanjiro Kamado, a kind-hearted boy whose family is attacked by demons. After discovering that his sister Nezuko has been transformed into a demon, Tanjiro begins a dangerous journey to find a cure and avenge his family. He joins the Demon Slayer Corps and battles powerful demons alongside his friends Zenitsu and Inosuke. The anime is known for its stunning animation, emotional storytelling, intense action scenes, and memorable characters. Demon Slayer explores themes of family, courage, sacrifice, friendship, and determination, making it one of the most loved anime series worldwide.

My Hero Academia

My Hero Academia is a popular Japanese anime set in a world where most people possess special abilities called Quirks. The story follows Izuku Midoriya, a young boy born without powers who dreams of becoming a great hero. His life changes when the legendary hero All Might chooses him to inherit his powerful ability. Izuku joins U.A. High School, where he trains alongside other aspiring heroes and faces dangerous villains. The anime is known for its exciting action, inspiring characters, emotional moments, and powerful battles. My Hero Academia explores themes of courage, friendship, hard work, responsibility, and the true meaning of being a hero.

Rating: 8.2/10
